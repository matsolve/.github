# MatSOLVE

**Material Simulation and Optimization Lab with Virtual Engineering**
Division of AI Convergence Research, Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology (KICET), Jinju, Korea

We build atomistic and data-driven models of functional ceramics: density functional theory for mechanisms, machine learning for prediction, and both together for deciding what to make next.

**Research topics**
- High-entropy oxides
- Dielectric ceramics for MLCCs
- Energy-conversion surfaces (solid-oxide cells, water splitting)
- Geopolymer and inorganic composites
